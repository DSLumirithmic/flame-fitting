Sections

Click adds a point to the active section, extending the line at whichever end is nearer (trace it corner to corner); Shift+click inserts a point into the nearest segment. A corner keeps one point. A few points per lid are enough (e.g. 5-9); the line between them is what gets scored. Drag a point to move it. Right-click a point to delete it. Wheel zooms at the cursor; middle-drag or Space+drag pans. 1-5 select section, U unclear, O occluded, Ctrl+Z undo, N/P next/previous view.

Upper lid: the visible edge where the upper lid margin (lash line) meets the eyeball.
Lower lid: the visible edge where the lower lid margin meets the eyeball — the lid, not the bottom of the iris.
Corners: where the upper and lower margins meet (inner = medial end of the opening).
Lip line: where the upper and lower lips meet, commissure to commissure.
Mark only what you can see. If part of a boundary is uncertain, either mark the visible part or flag the section Unclear / Occluded — flagged sections are excluded from scoring. Everything saves automatically. Coordinates are stored in original image pixels (origin at the top-left corner of the image).
